# Roadmap

The umbrella [Certification Coach roadmap](https://github.com/sviera91/certification-coach/blob/main/ROADMAP.md)
is maintained with the landing page in
[`sviera91/certification-coach`](https://github.com/sviera91/certification-coach).
That is the place for cross-platform proposals, including Azure, Terraform,
Google Cloud, and AWS. They remain future ideas, not available exam banks.

This repository owns **GitHub** practice: its canvas, Lite, question banks,
human reviews, translations, and GitHub-specific bugs and improvements.
Use [this repository's issues](https://github.com/sviera91/github-certification-coach/issues)
for those topics and [Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md) for question validation.
Future platform implementations will have their own repositories and review
workflows.
